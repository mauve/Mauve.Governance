# Contributor and agent entry points

> **Managed file: do not edit directly.** Governance updates replace this
> document. Propose framework-wide changes to the
> [original Mauve.Governance Policy](https://github.com/mauve/Mauve.Governance/blob/main/src/templates/docs/governance.md);
> keep repository-specific guidance outside managed paths.

Follow the installed [Mauve.Governance Policy](governance.md)
and the [style guide](style-guide.md). The installed policy
links to the canonical framework policy and describes which obligations the
selected profile actually enforces. For repositories with feature documents,
follow the lifecycle under `docs/Requirements/`; the `core` profile does not
require that directory to exist.

Files listed in `.mauve-governance/manifest.json` are managed. Review
Governance update proposals rather than editing installed copies; keep
repository-specific guidance outside managed paths. Do not treat planned
requirements, skipped tests, or source-only traceability as passing test-run
evidence. A documented rule is not an enforced check until its validator
and required gate exist.
